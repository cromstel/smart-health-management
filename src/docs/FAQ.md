# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This section answers some of the most common questions about the Smart Health Manager application.

## General

### What is Smart Health Manager?

Smart Health Manager is a comprehensive hospital management application designed to streamline various administrative and clinical tasks, including patient management, appointments, billing, and more.

### Who can use this application?

The application is designed for use by hospital staff, including doctors, nurses, receptionists, and administrators. Access to different features is determined by the user's role.

## Account and Login

### I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

You can reset your password by clicking on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. You will receive an email with instructions on how to create a new password.

### How do I change my email address?

To change your email address, please contact your system administrator.

## Technical

### Is my data secure?

Yes, we take data security very seriously. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and we follow industry best practices to protect your information.

### Can I access the application on my mobile device?

Yes, the application is designed to be responsive and can be accessed from any modern web browser on a desktop, tablet, or mobile device.
